'''Proto-Romance''' is the result of applying the [[comparative method]] to [[Linguistic reconstruction|reconstruct]] the latest common ancestor of the [[Romance languages]]. The closest real-life counterpart to Proto-Romance would have been a colloquial variety of [[Late Latin]] sometimes referred to as [[Vulgar Latin]].

==== Diphthong ====
{{IPA|/au̯/}} appears to be the only phonemic diphthong that can be reconstructed.<ref>{{harvcolnb|Ferguson|1976|p=84}}; {{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2015|p=81}}</ref>

==== Phonetics ====
* Vowels were lengthened in stressed open syllables.<ref>{{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2015|pp=118‒9}}; {{harvcolnb|Loporcaro|2015}}</ref>
* Stressed {{IPA|/ɛ ɔ/}} may have yielded incipient [[diphthong]]s like {{IPA|[e͡ɛ o͡ɔ]}} in [[Metaphony (Romance languages)|metaphonic]] conditions.<ref>{{harvcolnb|Ferguson|1976|p=chapter 7}}</ref>{{efn|That is, when followed by a syllable containing a [[close vowel]]. |name=|group=lower-roman}}
** Metaphony, if it can be projected back to Proto-Romance, may have initially been limited to open syllables. That is, it would have targeted allophonically lengthened {{IPA|/ɛ ɔ/}}.<ref>{{harvcolnb|Maiden|2016}}</ref>

==== Constraints ====
* {{IPA|/ɛ ɔ/}} did not occur in unstressed position.<ref name=":1">{{harvcolnb|Ferguson|1976|p=76}}; {{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2015|pp=78–81, 121–2}}</ref>
* {{IPA|/i u/}} did not occur in the second syllable of words with the structure [[Syllable#Transcription|ˌσσˈσσ]].<ref>{{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2015|pp=78–9}}</ref>{{efn|Diachronically this reflects the ‘weakening’ of vowels in this context, for which see {{harvcolnb|Lausberg|1970|p=§§292–6}}. An example, per the latter, is Latin {{lang|la|dorm'''ī'''torium}} > French {{lang|fr|dortoir}}.|name=|group=lower-roman}}

==== Palatalized consonants ====
{{See also|Palatalization in the Romance languages#/Cj/}}
* There is scholarly disagreement over whether palatalization was phonemic in Proto-Romance.<ref>{{harvcolnb|Operstein|2010|p=107}}</ref>{{efn|In representing it as such this article follows {{harvcolnb|Burger|1955}} and {{harvcolnb|Petrovici|1956}}. Similarly, {{harvcolnb|van den Bussche|1985}} proposes a Proto-Romance consonant inventory with /ʎʎ ɲɲ (t)tʲ (d)dʲ (k)kʲ (ɡ)ɡʲ/ (p. 226) and {{harvcolnb|Pope|1952}} reconstructs Proto-Gallo-Romance with a series of palatalized consonants (§168). {{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2015}} prefers a phonetic palatalization rule for Proto-Romance, as in /basiˈare/ [baˈsʲaːɾe] (p. 83).|name=|group=lower-roman}}
* Palatalized consonants tended to [[Gemination|geminate]] between vowels. The extent of this varied by consonant.<ref>{{harvcolnb|Lausberg|1970|p=§§451–478}}; {{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2015|pages=95, 111, 115}}; {{harvcolnb|Repetti|2016|p=659}}; {{harvcolnb|Barbato|2022|p=§1}}</ref>''{{efn|Gouvert assumes regular (phonetic) gemination of palatalized intervocalic /n l k/ to [ɲɲ ʎʎ cc]. Repetti points out that there exists (mixed) Romance evidence for the gemination of all consonants in this context other than original /s/.|name=|group=lower-roman}}''
* {{IPA|/tʲ/}} would have been an [[Voiceless alveolar affricate|affricate]] like {{IPA|[t͡sʲ]}}<ref>{{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2015|pp=86, 92}}</ref> or {{IPA|[t͡zʲ]}}.<ref>{{harvcolnb|Lausberg|1970|p=§452}}</ref>

==== Phonetics ====
* {{IPA|/sC/}} in word-initial position was assigned a [[Prothesis (linguistics)|prop-vowel]] [ɪ], as in {{IPA|/ˈstare/}} {{IPA|[ɪsˈtaːɾe]}}.<ref>{{harvcolnb|Lausberg|1970|p=§353}}; {{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2015|pp=125–6}}</ref>''{{efn|Example from Gouvert. Per Lausberg the prop-vowel would have been added only after a consonant or pause.|name=|group=lower-roman}}''
*{{IPA|/ɡn/}} was likely {{IPA|[ɣn]}} at first, with later developments varying by region.<ref>{{harvcolnb|Lausberg|1970|p=§444–8}}; {{harvcolnb|Chambon|2013}} apud {{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2015|p=95}}; {{harvcolnb|Zampaulo|2019|p=80–2}}</ref>''{{efn|Lausberg supposes an initial [ɣn~i̯n].|name=|group=lower-roman}}''
* {{IPA|/d ɡ/}} might have been fricatives or approximants between vowels.<ref>{{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2016|p=48}}</ref>
* {{IPA|/ll/}} might have been [[Voiced retroflex lateral approximant|retroflex]].<ref>{{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2015|p=15}}</ref>''{{efn|For further discussion on /ll/, see {{harvcolnb|Zampaulo|2019|p=71–7}} and {{harvcolnb|Lausberg|1970|p=§§494–9}}.|name=|group=lower-roman}}''
* {{IPA|/f/}} might have been [[Voiceless bilabial fricative|bilabial]].<ref>{{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2016|p=§1}}</ref>

==== Constraints ====
* {{IPA|/b/}} did not occur in intervocalic position.<ref>{{harvcolnb|Gouvert|2015|p=86}}</ref>{{efn|Diachronically this reflects the development of Latin intervocalic [b] to [β], and likewise [bj] to [βj], for which see {{harvcolnb|Lausberg|1970|p=§§366, 475}}.|name=|group=lower-roman}}

They are in agreement with Lausberg regarding the remaining inflections.|name=|group=lower-roman}}''
